Shipping & Duty

Made and shipped from India. Sold only in the UK and USA.

DDP, in plain words

DDP means Delivered Duty Paid. FLLAMS pays the import duty. The price you see is the price you pay. Price includes import duty. No extra charge on delivery. Nobody will ask you for money at the door.

Delivery

Orders are made and shipped from India. Delivery to the UK and USA takes an estimated 7–14 working days. You will receive tracking when your order ships.

Where we ship

UK and USA only. We do not ship within India.

Returns

If a piece does not fit, contact us within 14 days of delivery. We will arrange a return from the UK or USA. Returned pieces must be unworn, with tags on. Once the return arrives, we refund the price you paid. Return shipping from the UK or USA is arranged by FLLAMS — you do not pay import duty on a return.

1 of 1 pieces

1 of 1 pieces cannot be replaced if they sell out. If your size is gone, it is gone — but a custom piece on WhatsApp can be made in a similar cloth.
