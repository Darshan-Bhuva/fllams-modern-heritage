Your design, from a saree.

Custom pieces are not bought by checkout. They start as a conversation on WhatsApp. No prices here, no cart, no size selector — just three steps.

  1. Choose a cloth style. Tell us the kind of saree cloth you like — deep green, maroon, navy, gold border, or a colour you have in mind. We will show you what is available.
  2. Send your measurements. Chest, length, sleeve, and how you like a shirt to fit — relaxed, like the collection, or closer to the body. Use the Size Guide as a starting point.
  3. Approve the design. We send you the design — collar, closure, where the border sits — and make it only once you approve it.
